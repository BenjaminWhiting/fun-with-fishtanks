# Risers

Lift a Hygger light 5" on a rimless tank with 8 mm glass. Each riser clamps over the glass and carries a copy of
the glass edge on top, so the light's own bracket sits on it as it sat on the tank.
Story and pictures: https://benjaminwhiting.github.io/fun-with-fishtanks/projects/risers.html

- `hygger_riser.scad`: both designs. `part` = `riser`, `test_bottom`, `test_top` (fit tests), `gate_legs`,
  `gate_beam`, `gate` (assembly preview). `lift_in` sets the lift (3 to 5"); `gauge_slot` the glass slot.
- `glass_gauge.scad`: eight slots, 7.6 to 9.0 mm. Print it first and use the slot that slides on without rocking.
- `stl/`: the 5" bonsai riser (and earlier 3" and 4" lifts), the fit tests, the gauge, the gate's legs and beam.
- `print/`: sliced for a Bambu Lab A1 mini. Riser 4 h 45 m, 108 g PETG. Gate legs 2 h 48 m, 62 g; beam (three
  pieces and pins) 6 h 04 m, 147 g.

Print upright as modelled (slot down), no supports.
